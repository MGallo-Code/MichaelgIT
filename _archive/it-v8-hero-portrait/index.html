What I Help With

The everyday stuff, and then some.

Most calls fall into one of these three buckets. If yours doesn't, that's fine, just ask. I'd rather hear about it than have you guess whether it's something I do.

01 / Computers

Computers, top to bottom.

Setting up a new laptop, transferring files from an old one, sorting out a slow machine, removing things that shouldn't be there, getting printers and monitors talking to each other.

02 / Networks

Wi-Fi and networks.

Wi-Fi that drops in the back bedroom, routers that need replacing, mesh setups, password resets, smart home devices that won't connect, basic security cleanup.

03 / Websites

Small business websites.

A simple, professional site for your business or practice. Or a tune-up of one you already have. Built clean, hosted reliably, easy for you to update.

Whatever else, just ask. Phones, tablets, email troubles, smart TVs, backups, password managers. If I can help, I will. If it's not my thing, I'll tell you and point you somewhere good.
