Why I do this.

There's a particular kind of frustration that comes with feeling like the world has moved on without you. New phone, new login, new prompt asking you to "verify" something. I've watched smart, capable people quietly decide they're "bad at computers" because no one ever sat down with them and explained things in plain English.

I love the moment when that flips. When someone realizes the machine isn't smarter than them, it's just unfamiliar. Once it's familiar, it's yours.
